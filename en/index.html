Frequently asked questions

Where is my baby's diary stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There's no account, and we don't receive your diary, your baby's profile or photo.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud backup or a Mac or PC backup, or transfer data directly from your old iPhone: the diary is part of your iPhone backup.

Can two parents keep one diary?

Not yet: the diary lives on one iPhone and doesn't sync between devices. If this matters to you, let us know.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and find NomNom. In the app, the gear icon on the Home tab → Widgets shows what each widget does and how to add it.

Reminders don't arrive. What should I check?

Make sure notifications for NomNom are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → NomNom, and that the reminders you need are turned on in the app: gear icon on the Home tab → Notifications. Some reminders come only when there's a reason — for example, an allergen repeat comes two days after a trial.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone: the diary, profile and photo are deleted with it. Copies may remain in iPhone backups made earlier.

Does NomNom replace a pediatrician?

No. The app provides general information about starting solids. If you have questions about your baby's health or allergies, talk to your pediatrician.
